## English

**Subject:** Firefox add-on using Tureng — request for permission

Hello,

I have built a Firefox add-on that looks words up in the Tureng dictionary. Before
publishing it on the Firefox add-ons store (addons.mozilla.org), I would like to
ask your permission.

**What it does.** When the user selects a word on a page, or types one into the
add-on's search box, it fetches `tureng.com/en/turkish-english/<word>` once and
shows the results table to that user.

**Impact on your servers:**

- Every request is triggered directly by a user search. There is no crawling, no
  bulk downloading, and no database being built.
- Results are cached locally in the user's own browser (last 200 searches), so
  looking up the same word again sends no new request. In practice the add-on
  generates **less** traffic than the same user browsing your site by hand.
- No content is stored, reproduced, or redistributed.
- Every result view links back to tureng.com, sending users to your site for the
  full list.
- It is free, ad-free, and non-commercial.
- The full source is open and available for your review:
  **[GITHUB REPO LINK]**

**My request.** I have read in your Terms of Use that automated scripts for
extracting data are prohibited, and that "Tureng" may not be used without written
authorization. So I would like to ask your written permission for two things:

1. That the add-on may fetch and display your pages for user-initiated lookups only.
2. That the add-on may use the name "Tureng".

I am glad to adjust anything you are not comfortable with — rename the add-on,
show a prominent "unofficial, not affiliated with Tureng" notice, or rate-limit
requests.

Also: do you offer an official API or a partner programme I could use instead of
fetching pages? I would prefer that if it exists.

If you would rather not grant permission, I will not publish it and will keep it
on my own machine only.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
